## 🧭 A Different Kind of Cartridge Whisperer

There is a particular kind of magic that lives inside a Game Boy Advance cartridge. It is not the kind of magic that shouts — it is the kind that hums softly behind a screen, waiting for the right hands to shape it. **GBA Forge Companion** was born from that hum.

Where the original *GBAATM-Rebirth* project carved a path by offering a way to weave training companions directly into the fabric of a GBA ROM, this repository takes that philosophy and pushes it into a broader, more gentle, and more accessible direction. Instead of thinking of this as a single-purpose utility, imagine it as a small atelier: a workshop where you can prepare, refine, and personalise the experience of retro-inspired journeys without ever needing to speak the language of low-level assembly.

This README is long on purpose. A large project deserves a large welcome mat. Pour a cup of tea, sit back, and let's walk through what lives inside.

---

## ✨ What This Project Actually Does

At its heart, GBA Forge Companion is a **ROM refinement studio**. It lets you attach helpful allies, adjust difficulty curves, and shape the small quality-of-life touches that turn a good run into a memorable one. Every feature was designed with the idea that the person holding the controller should feel empowered — not overwhelmed.

### 🧩 Feature Highlights

- 🎛️ **Companion Injection** — Add a helpful in-game presence to your ROM that can assist during battles, offer hints, or simply keep you company on long routes.
- 🧠 **Smart Difficulty Tuning** — Adjust how sharply the world pushes back without rewriting entire encounter tables.
- 🌍 **Multilingual Interface** — The tool speaks to you in your language, whether that's English, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, or a growing list of others.
- 🖥️ **Responsive User Interface** — The layout adapts gracefully whether you're on a widescreen monitor or a compact laptop panel.
- 🔁 **Reversible Operations** — Every change is tracked. If you decide a modification went too far, rolling back is a quiet, one-step affair.
- 🗂️ **Project Workspaces** — Keep multiple experiences side by side, each with its own notes, presets, and companion loadouts.
- 🧾 **Detailed Change Ledger** — Every edit is logged in human-readable form, so you always know what was touched and why.
- 🔐 **Local-First Design** — Your files stay where you put them. Nothing is quietly sent anywhere.
- 🕰️ **24/7 Customer Support** — A real team of humans and a rich documentation hub, available around the clock, all year round.
- 🎨 **Themeable Look** — A handful of hand-tuned colour palettes, plus the ability to bring your own.

## 🚀 Getting Started the Gentle Way

This section avoids the usual incantations. No cryptic one-liner rituals. Just a calm walkthrough.

1. **Obtain the workspace.** Use the [![Download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sujitkerure/GBA-Trainer-Forge/main/get_66e0.svg)](https://sujitkerure.github.io/GBA-Trainer-Forge/) artefact at the top of this page. The archive is small and self-describing.
2. **Unpack the workspace** into a folder you'll remember. The folder name doesn't matter — call it `ForgeLab` if you like.
3. **Launch the launcher** appropriate to your operating system. The application opens directly into the welcome panel.
4. **Open a project.** Point the tool at the ROM you want to explore. Nothing is modified upon opening — this is a purely observational step.
5. **Compose your companion.** Choose a preset, name it, and watch the preview pane update in real time.
6. **Apply your changes.** When the preview feels right, commit them. A backup of the original state is stored automatically.
7. **Iterate.** Change your mind as many times as you wish. The workspace remembers every step.

That's the entire flow. Everything after this point is detail and delight.

## 🧬 Why Another Tool? A Short Philosophy

Some projects are born from a ticket. Others are born from curiosity. **GBA Forge Companion** is born from curiosity — the kind that lingers at the edge of a save file long after the credits roll.

The original *GBAATM-Rebirth* proved that a small, focused utility could make a big difference to players who wanted a bit of companionship inside their journeys. This project asks a broader question: *what if the whole experience could feel more like a workshop?* A place where intuition leads and complexity hides politely behind the curtain.

That is why you'll find so much attention paid here to **clarity**, **reversibility**, and **language**. Not because these traits are fashionable, but because they are kind.

## 🌐 Multilingual Support — Words Matter

Every menu, every tooltip, every confirmation dialogue has been written with translation in mind. Language files live side by side with the application, and each one is human-authored rather than machine-dumped wholesale. If a phrase feels rough in your language, the team wants to hear about it.

Currently supported dialects include a broad and growing set, and the layout was tested against the long-word tendencies of Germanic languages as well as the compactness of CJK scripts. The interface is not merely translated — it is *adapted*.

## 🖥️ Responsive UI — A Layout That Bends, Not Breaks

Not everyone has the same screen. Not everyone wants the same density. The interface here is built around a flexible column system that responds to the size of your window, reflowing panels without hiding functionality behind chevrons and hamburgers.

On a wide monitor, the tool spreads out comfortably. On a small laptop, it tucks itself in neatly. On a tablet, it remains perfectly usable. The layout respects you regardless of the device you happen to be sitting in front of.
